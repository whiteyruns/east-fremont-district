BOOK THE BLOCK — THE EAST FREMONT DISTRICT
Thirteen venues, one street, one contract. Private for a night, a week, or a full program.

The Las Vegas Convention & Visitors Authority is sharing this with the planners
who bring the world to Las Vegas. We're the team behind the East Fremont
District, known as F.E.E.D.: a single Downtown block, thirteen venues, run by
one operator, that you can close to the public and take over for your group.

For a night, a week, or a full program, the block is yours. The street closes,
every venue goes private, and your guests are the only people on it. Permits,
security, F&B, production and staffing run through one team under one contract.

ONE BLOCK. THIRTEEN VENUES. YOUR GUESTS ONLY.
F.E.E.D. is built for private programs at a scale a single venue can't hold:
welcome receptions, executive dinners, incentive nights, awards, convention
off-sites, for 200 to 10,000+ guests across rooftops, clubs, restaurants and a
closed street.

  • Private by default — the street closes, the venues go private, access is
    credentialed. No walk-ins, no other events.
  • One contract, one operator — permits, security, F&B, production, staffing
    and reporting, from one accountable team.
  • Room for any group — 61,600 sq ft indoors, 15,000 capacity with the street
    closed, venues from a 99-seat showroom to a 1,000-guest rooftop club.
  • Downtown, close to everything — minutes from the Strip and the Convention
    Center.
